## 4. Today's fix — the daily check could not see the kill switch (`scripts/trade-quality.mjs`)

**The defect.** The app keeps two daily records whose names are one letter apart: `state.daily` is
`{date, count}`, the trade counter, and `state.dailyPnl` is `{date, realized, tripped}`, the kill switch
(`autoTrader.ts:4306` `killSwitchCheck`, `autoTrader.ts:4284` `rollDaily`).
`scripts/trade-quality.mjs:24` printed `kill ${s.daily?.tripped ? 'TRIPPED' : 'clear'}` — a field that does
not exist on that object. **The line could only ever print "clear."** This morning it printed "clear" while
the trader had been halted for two and a half hours, and it is the line this maintenance session reads to
decide whether the trader is healthy. That is a monitoring blindness in the loop that is supposed to catch
exactly this, and it is why the halt took forty minutes of digging instead of one line of output.

**The fix, smallest diff.** A new pure module `scripts/lib/kill-state.mjs` exporting `killState(state,
today)`; `trade-quality.mjs` imports it, prints its text, and pushes a FLAG when tripped. It reads
`dailyPnl`, honours the same UTC-day guard the app uses (a trip is scoped to its own day and cleared on the
roll), and reports **whichever ledger is worse**, venue or local, the way `dayRealizedForKill` chooses it.
**No app source was touched** — this is a reporting script, so there is nothing to restart and no risk to
the money path.

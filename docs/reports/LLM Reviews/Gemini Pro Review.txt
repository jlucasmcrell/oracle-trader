Strongest disagreement
The operator's assertion in the TRADE-HISTORY report that the fade strategy has "No edge; the profit is inside noise" because its win rate matches its entry prices is wrong. This assumes that all edge must be directional. Because fade operates as a MAKER (fadeEntryMode = 'maker'), it buys below fair value by capturing the spread and avoiding taker fees. Winning exactly 95% of the time at 0.95 fair-value prices with a maker queue position yields a mathematically positive Expected Value (+5.9¢ per $1 staked in your own numbers). The spread capture is the edge, and the strategy should not be sidelined purely for lacking a predictive directional signal.
